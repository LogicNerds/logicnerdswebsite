Behind the scenes

  • Tracks each customer's status, lifetime value, most recent win and renewal date automatically.
  • Counts open deals on every account, so customers with nothing in the pipeline stand out.

Everything the pack adds is labeled so it can't collide with what's already in your org, and any of it can be switched off.

Easy to adjust

  • How long a customer counts as active, and how renewals are timed, can be adjusted if your contracts aren't annual.
