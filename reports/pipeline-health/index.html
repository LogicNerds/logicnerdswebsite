Behind the scenes

  • Tracks how long each deal has gone without activity, so stale deals surface on their own.
  • Ensures deals at 20% probability or beyond have the data you need to measure success.
  • Keeps open deals from being moved to a close date that has already passed.

Everything the pack adds is labeled so it can't collide with what's already in your org, and any of it can be switched off.

Easy to adjust

  • The stale and stuck thresholds can be changed to match your sales cycle.
  • The 20% line can move to wherever deals become real in your stages.
