Rules and automation

Everything here is active when the pack installs. Any of it can be switched off in Setup if you don't want it.

  • Validation rule: open deals at 20% probability or higher need an Amount and a Next Step before they can be saved.
  • Validation rule: open deals can't be given a close date in the past when the close date or stage changes. Deals already past due still save and show up on the Deals Past Close Date report.

Easy to adjust

  • Stale Deals uses 14 days and Stuck in Stage uses 30. Change either number in the report filter.
  • The Amount and Next Step rule starts at 20% probability. Move that to wherever deals become real in your stages.
