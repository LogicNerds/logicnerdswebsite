Pipeline Intelligence

The report packs show where your pipeline is today. Pipeline Intelligence shows how it moves: where deals stall, which close dates keep slipping, and how long leads take to become real opportunities.

Includes all four report packs. Professional Edition and up.

Screenshot: Pipeline Movement dashboard

What it answers

  • Which stages do deals stall in, and where do they move backward?
  • What percentage of deals make it from each stage to the next?
  • How often do close dates slip, and which reps push them most?
  • How much do deal amounts change between when a deal is created and when it closes?
  • How long does a lead take to go from marketing-qualified to sales-qualified?
  • How much of the pipeline you create actually turns into revenue?

What it tracks

  • Records every stage a deal passes through, how long it stayed and where it went next.
  • Tracks how many times each deal's close date has been pushed and how far its amount has moved.
  • Records when each lead reaches each stage of your funnel, based on the lead statuses you already use.

Guardrails that keep the data honest

Pipeline Intelligence keeps close dates current and makes sure lost deals and disqualified leads get a reason, so the movement data is worth trusting. The guardrails are on from the day you install. Any of them can be switched off if your team isn't ready.

What's included

  • All four report packs: Pipeline Health, Win/Loss & Velocity, Leads & Top of Funnel and Accounts & Activity
  • Reports on deal movement, stage conversion, slippage and lead progression that standard Salesforce data can't produce
  • A Pipeline Movement dashboard
  • Rep-Level Personal reports, including a stale-deal list each rep can have emailed to them
  • A setup guide, including how to adjust or switch off each guardrail
