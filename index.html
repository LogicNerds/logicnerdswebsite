Built to stay out of your way

Your org already has years of fields, reports and habits in it. The packs are built so nothing collides with what's there.

  • Every field the packs add has a label ending in (RP), so you can always tell them apart from yours. The install guide shows how to relabel them if you'd rather not see it.

Why start with reporting

For most small teams, the first real problem is that nobody can see what's going on. CRM data is usually in worse shape than people assume. When researchers had 75 executives check the last 100 records their own teams created, only 3% of the results met a basic quality bar. Analyst estimates put the share of CRM projects that miss their objectives at roughly half.

Good reports are the cheapest way to find out where your data is thin and where deals are stalling, before you pay anyone to rebuild anything.

Questions

Which Salesforce editions do the packs work with?

Professional Edition and up. Confirm whether Starter Suite and Pro Suite are supported.

Will installing a pack change or overwrite anything in my org?

No. Everything installs under the LNRP_ prefix with (RP) labels, and nothing in a pack edits your existing fields, reports or records.

Do I need a Salesforce admin to install it?

You need someone with admin permissions in your org, but not a dedicated admin. The install guide walks through every step. Most installs take time.

Are there any automations?

No. The packs are reports, dashboards and a small number of supporting fields. Nothing runs in the background.

Can I change the reports after I install them?

Yes. Once installed, they're regular reports and dashboards in your org. Edit them, clone them or delete what you don't need.

I bought one pack. Can I upgrade to all four?

Upgrade policy: credit toward the $99 bundle or not.
