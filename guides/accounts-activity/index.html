Weekly and monthly rhythm

  • Weekly (about 15 minutes): check Upcoming Renewals and Dark Accounts on the Accounts & Activity dashboard. These are the lists where waiting costs revenue.
  • Monthly: go through Customers With No Open Opportunity and Top Customers, and pick the accounts each rep will work next month.
  • Quarterly: clean up the data gaps on the two Missing reports.

Upcoming Renewals

Active customers whose renewal date falls in the next 90 days, soonest first. The renewal date is the yearly anniversary of their first won deal.

What to do

  • Start the renewal conversation as soon as an account appears on this list, not in the last week.
  • Create a renewal opportunity for each one so it shows up in your pipeline and forecast.
  • Check each account's last activity. A renewal that's also on the Dark Accounts list needs attention first.

Watch for

The pack assumes annual contracts. If yours aren't annual, the dates will be off. The install guide covers what to do instead.

Dark Accounts

Current and past customers with no logged activity in 90 days or more, or none ever, largest first. If nobody has talked to a customer in three months, that's where churn and missed expansion start.

What to do

  • Each owner contacts the top accounts on their list this week and logs the activity.
  • Work the list from the top. It's sorted by lifetime value, so the biggest risk comes first.
  • If one owner has far more dark accounts than the rest, they may have more accounts than they can cover. Rebalance.

Watch for

Activity only counts if it's logged in Salesforce. An account your team talks to every week can still show as dark if those calls aren't logged.

Customers With No Open Opportunity

Active and lapsed customers with nothing open in the pipeline, highest lifetime value first.

What to do

  • Active customers here are expansion opportunities nobody is pursuing. Pick the top few for each rep and set a task to find out what else they need.
  • Lapsed customers here are win-back targets. They already know you. A short check-in about what changed often costs less than a new lead.
  • Not every account needs an open deal. The point is to decide on purpose, not by default.

Top Customers by Lifetime Won Amount

Your accounts ranked by total closed-won amount, with their status and last activity.

What to do

  • Make sure every top customer has recent activity. Losing one of these costs more than any new deal is likely to bring in.
  • Look for what your best customers have in common, such as industry, size or how they found you. Use that to decide which prospects to go after.

Activity by Rep

Tasks and events dated this month, open or completed, by the rep they're assigned to.

What to do

  • Read it next to results, not on its own. High activity with few wins is a different conversation than low activity with steady wins.
  • Very low numbers usually mean a rep isn't logging their work, not that they aren't working. Every other report in the pack depends on logged activity, so fix that first.

Watch for

Early in the month this chart is small. Compare at the same point each month.

Accounts Missing Key Info and Contacts Missing Email or Title

Accounts missing Industry, Employees or Billing Country, and contacts missing an email or title, by owner.

What to do

  • Give each owner their own list and 15 minutes a week to work through it.
  • Make the fields required on page layouts going forward, so the list stops growing.
  • Fix contacts on your top customers first. A missing title on a decision maker matters more than one on an old contact.

Watch for

Missing account data breaks segmentation. Reporting by industry, company size or region only works when those fields are filled in.

Rep-Level Personal reports

My Accounts and My Upcoming Renewals show each rep only their own accounts.

What to do

  • Have each rep subscribe to My Upcoming Renewals weekly, set to send only when there's at least one record. The install guide shows how.
  • My Accounts is sorted with the stalest accounts first. Reps can use it to plan who to contact each week.
